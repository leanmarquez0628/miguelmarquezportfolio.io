About Me

I'm Lean Miguel A. Marquez, a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering graduate of Camarines Norte State Colleges (2020–2025), based in Daet, Camarines Norte.

During my internship at the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) District Engineering Office (District II) in June–July 2024, I got a first look at real public works. My capstone project was the design and analysis of a three-storey GSIS building in Sitio Mat-E, Barangay Santo Domingo, Vinzons, Camarines Norte. Along with architectural design services, I provide comprehensive cost estimation for construction projects, such as single-story residential structures.

Outside of engineering, I love drawing, learning web development and SQL, and studies in Economics, Programming and Data Analytics. Aside from this, I'm learning Digital Arts, Photography, and Cinematography.
